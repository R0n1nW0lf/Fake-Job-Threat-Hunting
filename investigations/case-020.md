# Case-020: TaskVerse (retitled Fortress role, requirements and pay stripped)

> **Verdict note:** This is a repackaging case: the poster, TaskVerse, took a real Fortress Information Security role — Security Risk Assessor, a third-party/vendor risk (TPRM) job — retitled it "Cyber Security Engineer", and stripped out the pay range and both stated requirements (2+ years proven experience, Required; Associate degree, Required). No role with the advertised title exists on Fortress's own board. TaskVerse is a repeat actor in this register: Case-015 and Case-017.

## Observation

A LinkedIn card under TaskVerse's name showed a Cyber Security Engineer role for Fortress Information Security, United States Remote, Full-time, no pay stated. The duties were primarily third-party and vendor risk management — vendor reviews and risk assessments — with vulnerability management and incident response adjacent work. No required years or held certifications were stated on the card. Because governance-heavy roles are the investigator's call rather than autopilot, the job watch held it (LH-9501, HELD / UNVERIFIED) and surfaced it; the investigator directed a dig on 2026-11-11.

## Hypothesis

Either TaskVerse was posting a real Fortress Cyber Security Engineer opening through an authorized channel, or the card repackaged a different, real Fortress role under a more attractive title with the disqualifying details removed. Verification checked Fortress's own board for the advertised title and for the closest matching real role, and compared title, duties, pay, requirements, and location between the two versions.

## Evidence

- **No role with the advertised title exists at the named employer.** Fortress's own board has no Cyber Security Engineer role at all. The closest real role is Security Risk Assessor, the vendor risk job — the same TPRM work the TaskVerse card describes.
- **The retitle changes the job family.** "Cyber Security Engineer" reads as hands-on detection/engineering work. The real role is a Security Risk Assessor doing third-party risk management — governance-side work, a materially different job than the title advertises.
- **Both stated requirements were stripped.** Fortress's own listing marks at least 2 years of proven experience as Required, plus an Associate degree, also Required. The TaskVerse card states neither. Under the investigator's standing rule, a Required qualification he does not meet is an automatic skip — removing it from the card is what made this posting look eligible.
- **The pay was stripped.** The real role pays $67,832–$94,212 — its bottom sits below the investigator's $80,000 floor. The TaskVerse card lists no pay at all, so the floor could not be checked from the card.
- **Location was loosened.** The card says Remote, US. The real role sits as Orlando HQ Remote, Florida, and Michigan eligibility is nowhere stated in Fortress's own listing.
- **Repeat actor, third case.** TaskVerse is the poster in Case-015 (a paraphrase of a real CrowdStrike role with its requirements and pay stripped) and Case-017 (a swapped-template Cyber Security Engineer whose twin described an unrelated German food company). The pattern is consistent across all three: real employers' material, repackaged, with the details that would disqualify or cool interest removed.
- **No fee or payment request.** No fee, training, or payment request was found in the posting text.

## Verification

- Posting review: LinkedIn 4489958651 logged by the job watch 2026-11-11 (LH-9501) — TPRM-leaning duties, Engineer title, pay undisclosed, no required years or certs stated on the card.
- Named-employer check: Fortress's own board checked at dig time (2026-11-11) — no Cyber Security Engineer role; closest real role is Security Risk Assessor.
- Comparison check: Fortress's own listing for Security Risk Assessor (also sighted directly as LinkedIn 4481156403) — $67,832–$94,212, Greater Orlando Remote, at least 2 years proven experience Required, Associate degree Required.
- Poster history check: this register, Case-015 and Case-017 — same poster, same repackaging pattern.
- No application attempted; no personal information sent.

## Conclusion

**Not verified as the opening it advertises — a real employer's real role, retitled and stripped of its pay and both requirements by a repeat aggregator front. Not proven to be a data-harvesting scam.** The underlying Fortress job exists, which keeps this weaker than Case-019's false-employer-body verdict. What fails is the card itself: the title names a job Fortress does not have, and the two facts that would have closed it on sight for this investigator — the Required 2 years and the pay range starting below his floor — are exactly the facts the card omits. Read with Case-015 and Case-017, TaskVerse has now carried three postings in this register built on other employers' material with material details removed. Who operates the repackaging, and what applicant information sent through it is used for, is Not Verified.

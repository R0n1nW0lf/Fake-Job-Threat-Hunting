## Observation

A LinkedIn card under TaskVerse's name showed a System Engineer role, remote US, pay not shown. The posting text was CrowdStrike Systems Engineer boilerplate under a different poster's name, and the duties read as production-systems / SRE work rather than cybersecurity analysis, so the real employer and the role's fit could not be verified from the posting. It was held for Ronnie's call.

## Hypothesis

Either TaskVerse is an authorized channel for the CrowdStrike role, or it is an aggregator repost that stripped the source posting's specifics. Verification compared the body against CrowdStrike's real role and checked what TaskVerse actually is.

## Evidence

- **The poster is an aggregator front.** TaskVerse is a storefront for FetchJobs.co, a job-board aggregator — not the employer named in the body.
- **The body is someone else's job, paraphrased.** A real CrowdStrike Systems Engineer II role exists (R29877, $100,000–$145,000, 3+ years required). The TaskVerse text is a stripped-down paraphrase of it, not an exact copy — and the specifics that matter were removed, including the 3+ years requirement and the pay range.
- **The duties are not cybersecurity analysis.** The work is production systems and SRE — keeping services running. "Incident response" there means restoring service, not security incidents.
- **The card is dead anyway.** At dig time the LinkedIn posting was no longer accepting applications.
- **Authorization unconfirmed.** No evidence TaskVerse/FetchJobs is authorized to repost CrowdStrike's role.

## Verification

- Poster identity check: TaskVerse → FetchJobs aggregator relationship established 2026-10-25.
- Source-text comparison: body matched against CrowdStrike Systems Engineer II (R29877) — paraphrase with requirements and pay stripped.
- Duties check: production-systems/SRE, outside cybersecurity-analysis scope.
- No application attempted; no personal information sent.

## Conclusion

**Not verified as an authorized posting — mislabeled repost. Not proven to be a data-harvesting scam.** The pattern matches Case-013 (Torentify): a third-party front reposting a real employer's job under its own name, with the details that would let an applicant judge the role removed.

## Action Taken

## Observation

A full-time Easy Apply posting titled "Information Security Analyst", listed as Moline, Illinois and tagged Remote, appeared under the company name Nexorant LLC. The description is a generic security-analyst template (monitoring security events and alerts, threat analysis, incident investigation, vulnerability assessments, remediation support, policy implementation, documentation) and refers only to "client environments"; no end employer or client is named anywhere. Requirements list cybersecurity / InfoSec knowledge, network security (firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPN), application security (OWASP Top 10), and SIEM / endpoint / vulnerability tools as "advantageous"; Security+, CySA+, and CEH are listed as "beneficial" (not required), and a bachelor's degree is preferred. No "Posted by" person was displayed (checked twice); the posting was made under the company account itself. At the investigation check the posting was under an hour old, still open, and LinkedIn displayed 3 applicants.

## Hypothesis

The posting may be a staffing firm's blind posting for a genuine but unnamed client, or it may be an unverifiable listing that should not receive personal information. The investigation set out to determine which, without assuming either.

## Evidence

- The posting describes unnamed "client environments"; no end employer or client is identified.
- The firm describes itself on LinkedIn as a "recruitment and career enablement firm focused on helping professionals and organizations navigate the US hiring market."
- The LinkedIn company page (linkedin.com/company/nexorant-llc/) lists: industry IT Services and IT Consulting; HQ Moline, IL 61265; founded 2022; size 51–200 employees with 44–45 associated members; approximately 2,739–3K followers; website nexorant.com. Its Jobs tab showed exactly one active job — this posting. Its posts target Indian job seekers (OPT, H-1B, visa interviews, entry-level remote job posts for "freshers", Diwali / Navratri greetings).
- The firm's website (nexorant.com) is a recruitment / staffing agency site claiming 500+ companies hiring and 12k+ active candidates. It does not offer cybersecurity services. It sells paid candidate services (IT training, resume building, interview support, talent pool) and has a Pricing page. Contact details shown: Moline, IL 61265, a +1 (415) phone number, and Contact@nexorant.com.
- HQ inconsistency: LinkedIn and the firm's website state Moline, IL, while a Dice company profile for the firm lists HQ as Chicago, IL. This is documented as an inconsistency, not as proof of fraud.

## Verification

- An exact search for "Information Security Analyst" + Nexorant returned zero results.
- A distinctive description phrase ("safeguarding information assets and ensuring the security posture of client environments") returned zero exact matches.
- A general search for "Nexorant LLC" returned no results about the company on Google.
- Other traces of the business were found: a Yahoo local listing (Moline, IL); a CB Insights profile (recruitment agency, founded 2022, HQ Moline — unclaimed); and a Dice company profile describing a staffing firm, with other active generic remote postings (for example, a "Data Analyst" role at $70K–$90K posted days earlier). The business therefore has a modest multi-platform footprint, but the specific advertised role has zero corroboration anywhere, including on the firm's own site, where listings sit behind a portal login.
- Equally, no evidence was found that the firm had copied another employer's job, impersonated a specific unrelated business, used a dead contact domain, or used an unaffiliated poster, which are the indicators behind the stronger verdicts in Cases 002, 003, 005, and 006. The posting account appears to be the firm's own, with branding consistent since 2022 and its own website and other-platform presence.

## Conclusion

**Not verified — skip. Not proven fake.** A traceable staffing business exists, but the end employer is unnamed, the role is uncorroborated, the HQ claims are inconsistent (Moline vs Chicago), and the firm markets paid services to candidates. The evidence supports "cannot be verified as a genuine role", not "fraudulent", and this case is recorded as unverifiable only.

## Action Taken

- Skipped. No application and no personal information submitted.
- Case kept separate from the confirmed impersonation pattern. Together with Case-004, it forms a second, distinct category in this register: unverifiable blind staffing posts, as opposed to impersonation of third-party companies.

## Indicators

- No corroboration for the role found anywhere outside the LinkedIn posting, including on the firm's own website
- Staffing-style text naming no end employer or client (only "client environments")
- HQ inconsistency between the firm's LinkedIn page and website (Moline, IL) and its Dice company profile (Chicago, IL)
- The firm markets paid services to candidates (IT training, resume building, interview support, talent pool) and has a Pricing page
- The firm's Jobs tab showed exactly one active job — this posting

## Indicators Absent (why the verdict is weaker)

- No copied job text from a real employer identified
- No impersonation of a specific unrelated business established; the account appears to be the firm's own, with consistent branding since 2022
- No poster affiliation mismatch (no poster was displayed; the posting was made under the company account itself)
- No dead contact domain (the contact email is on the firm's own domain)
- No rapid closure documented (the posting was still open at the investigation check, under an hour after posting, so closure behaviour could not be assessed)

# Evidence Register

This register records the evidence items behind the fifteen investigations in this repository: what was checked, where, when, how it was verified, and the limits of each item.

## How this evidence was gathered

All evidence was gathered from live public web sources during an active job search (October 2–25, 2026). Postings and company pages were reviewed as they appeared; most of the original pages have since closed or changed and can no longer be reviewed in their investigation-time state. Findings were recorded the same day as dated investigation notes with source URLs, which is why this register can state collection dates and verification methods even though no evidence files were preserved.

No screenshots were captured or retained for any item in this register. Because no evidence files exist, no SHA-256 hashes exist either; the hash column states this plainly rather than presenting a substitute. Likewise, no hash, screenshot filename, or timestamp has been reconstructed or estimated. Where an exact time, URL, or value was not recorded at investigation time, the register says "Not Verified".

Going forward, any preserved evidence file will be logged here with its filename, capture date, and SHA-256 hash at the time of collection.

## Reading this register

- **Observed / Finding** items state what was seen or what a check returned on the collection date.
- **Hypotheses** (for example, that a posting's purpose was information harvesting, or that a company page was hijacked) are recorded in the case files as hypotheses and are never presented in this register as established outcomes.
- Items marked **Not Verified** were not recorded at investigation time or could not be confirmed. They are listed so the gaps in the evidence are visible, not hidden.

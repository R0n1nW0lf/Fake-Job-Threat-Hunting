## Observation

A full-time remote posting for a "Junior Cybersecurity Analyst", listed in Pennsylvania, US, appeared under a company whose displayed name was literally "No aplica". The job description was a generic template that named no company, no contact email or phone, no pay, and no website. No job poster was displayed.

## Confirmed Observations

- The company page (linkedin.com/company/gestiuno-servicios-legales-s-c/) displayed the name "No aplica", while its URL slug named Gestiuno Servicios Legales, S.C.
- The page showed Law Practice as its industry, headquarters in San Pedro Garza Garcia, Nuevo Leon, Mexico, 0–1 employees, no website, and legal and real-estate specialties.
- The posting text contained no company name, contact details, pay, or website.
- No job poster was displayed.
- LinkedIn displayed 26 applicants, and the posting closed within approximately 7 hours.
- A second, unrelated LinkedIn company page, with URL slug "catelca", also displayed the name "No aplica".

## Hypothesis — Not Verified

The genuine Gestiuno page may have been renamed or hijacked without authorization and used as a shell for the posting. **This mechanism is Not Verified.** The investigation found no direct evidence of compromise, unauthorized renaming, an insider action, or who controls the page. Other possibilities, including a platform data error or repurposing of an abandoned page, cannot be excluded on the available evidence.

## Evidence

- The displayed company name was a placeholder rather than the name in the page URL slug.
- The page's industry, size, headquarters, and specialties were inconsistent with the advertised US cybersecurity role.
- The posting used a generic template naming no employer in the text.
- The real Gestiuno Servicios Legales, S.C. was identified in Mexican business directories as a small law office, with no cybersecurity offering, no US operations, and no careers page.
- The same placeholder display name appeared on a second, unrelated company's LinkedIn page.

## Verification

- Mexican business directories, including Cylex and Infobel, were used to identify the real firm's name, location, and line of business.
- Exact-phrase web searches for two distinctive lines from the description ("motivated and detail-oriented Junior Cybersecurity Analyst" and "develop practical experience while working alongside experienced IT and cybersecurity professionals") returned zero results. No corroboration for the role was found outside the LinkedIn posting.
- The second page display was checked through its LinkedIn URL slug ("catelca"); it also displayed "No aplica". The investigation did not establish who changed either display name or whether any change was authorized.

## Conclusion

**Likely fake / impersonation.** The verdict is based on the confirmed identity mismatch between the placeholder display name and the real firm named in the URL slug, the unrelated law-practice business profile, the generic employer-free template, the absence of a poster or contact path, the lack of corroboration outside LinkedIn, and the rapid closure with 26 applicants displayed.

The conclusion does **not** establish that the genuine firm's page was compromised, hijacked, or renamed without authorization. Page control and the renaming mechanism remain **Not Verified**.

## Action Taken

- Not applied. No personal information submitted.
- Future postings under a "No aplica" company display name are flagged for the same verification pattern on sight; the display name alone is not treated as proof of who controls the underlying page.

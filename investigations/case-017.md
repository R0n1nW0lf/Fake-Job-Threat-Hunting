# Case-017: TaskVerse (swapped-template Cyber Security Engineer)

> **Verdict note:** This is a swapped-template case: the poster is a job-board front, not an employer, its own careers page lists no openings, and the same generic body appears under the same title for another country with a company description for an unrelated German food company founded in 1958. TaskVerse also appears in Case-015 (a different posting) — this is a repeat-pattern actor across two cases in this register.

## Observation

A LinkedIn card under TaskVerse's name showed a Cyber Security Engineer role, United States Remote, Full-time, no pay stated, badged "Promoted by hirer · Responses managed off LinkedIn". The description was generic boilerplate with no specific duties, so the real employer and the actual work could not be verified from the posting. It was surfaced to the investigator, who directed a dig on 2026-11-03.

## Hypothesis

Either TaskVerse was posting a real employer's opening through an authorized channel, or the card was an aggregator/template posting with the company name rotated into boilerplate. Verification checked the posting body itself, TaskVerse's own site and careers page, the same title and template in another location, and the board of the employer the boilerplate is associated with (Leidos).

## Evidence

- **The body is a generic template, not a role.** The full description runs under generic headings — About The Company, About The Role, Qualifications, Responsibilities, Benefits, Equal Opportunity — with no role-specific duties, team, or location detail, no required years, and no required certifications. Its own text says details "vary by team" and "depend on the position and department", and that eligibility "may include" citizenship or clearance, with nothing stated for this role.
- **The posting accepts no applications.** It states "Not currently accepting applications" on its face. There is no Apply or Easy Apply button and no external apply destination to inspect.
- **The poster is a job board, not an employer.** TaskVerse's LinkedIn About lists its website as fetchjobs.co, which describes itself as a remote-job platform / job board. Its career page lists no specific openings, only a generic "Send Resume" prompt — the shape of a resume-collection front rather than a hiring employer. A FetchJobs search for this title returned other employers' aggregated listings and no TaskVerse-as-employer role in the first 50 results.
- **No matching posting on the named employer's own board.** On careers.leidos.com, an unfiltered search returned 249 "Cyber Security Engineer" results; filtering to Remote + United States returned only 4 jobs, none titled "Cyber Security Engineer" (Senior Lead Security Engineer — Arlington, VA, R-00195044; Security Architect — Bedford, MA, R-00194176; Senior AI Platform Engineer — Adelphi, MD, R-00192138; Network Architect — Bedford, MA, R-00194177). No Leidos posting matches this posting's title, generic description, and US-remote terms.
- **Template reuse is proven, not inferred.** A second TaskVerse LinkedIn posting with the identical title, "Cyber Security Engineer", Germany (Remote), job 4486832825, uses the same template — but its "About The Company" section describes apetito AG, a family-owned food company founded in 1958. The company section is swapped boilerplate, not a real description of any poster or employer.
- **Repeat actor.** TaskVerse is the same poster as in Case-015 (System Engineer, LinkedIn 4483385316, a paraphrase of a real CrowdStrike role with its requirements and pay stripped). The same front has now carried two unverifiable postings built on other employers' material.
- **No fee or payment request.** No fee, training, or payment request was found in the posting text.

## Verification

- Posting review: LinkedIn 4486493781 read in full at dig time (2026-11-03) — generic template, no role specifics, "Not currently accepting applications", 7 clicks displayed.
- Poster identity check: TaskVerse LinkedIn About → fetchjobs.co job-board relationship; FetchJobs.co and fetchjobs.co/career reviewed — a remote-job board whose career page lists no openings, "Send Resume" only.
- Template check: Germany twin posting (LinkedIn 4486832825) located with the identical title and template; its company section describes apetito AG, founded 1958.
- Named-employer check: careers.leidos.com searched at dig time — 249 unfiltered results for the title; Remote + United States filter returned 4 jobs, none matching.
- No application attempted; no personal information sent.

## Conclusion

**Not verified as a real opening from a real employer — a swapped-template posting from an aggregator front. Not proven to be a data-harvesting scam.** The defining indicators are the template rotation (the same body serving an unrelated German food company in another country), a careers page with no openings and only a resume prompt, a US card that accepts no applications, and no matching posting on the named employer's own board. Read together with Case-015, TaskVerse is a repeat-pattern actor in this register: an aggregator front whose postings borrow real employers' material without a verifiable opening behind them. Who operates the template rotation, and what it gains, is Not Verified.

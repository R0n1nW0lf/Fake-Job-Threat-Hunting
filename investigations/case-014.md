## Observation

A LinkedIn card showed a Security Analyst (L1) role under Blue Mantis. The listing contradicted itself on location (Remote tag vs Portsmouth, NH / Woodbridge, NJ passages), showed no pay, and blended L1 SOC duties with IT-support work (password resets, account lockouts, software installs). As a staffing/MSP posting, direct-vs-contract and the employer of record were also unresolved, so it was held for Ronnie's call.

## Hypothesis

The card may reflect a genuine client or internal role that closed quickly — or a listing with no live requisition behind it. Verification checked the employer's own board for the role.

## Evidence

- **The LinkedIn posting is already gone.** At dig time it returned removed or invalid, so no posting text remains to verify location, duties, or employer against.
- **The employer's own board has no such role.** Blue Mantis's careers board listed 37 jobs with no Security Analyst L1 on it at all. Its security listings were a Network Security Engineer and a Senior Network and Security Engineer — neither is this role.

## Verification

- LinkedIn posting ID 4483568063: removed/invalid at dig time, 2026-10-25.
- Blue Mantis careers board (Pinpoint): full check, 37 jobs, no matching role.
- No application attempted; no personal information sent; no account created.

## Conclusion

**Not verified — skip. Not proven fake.** A LinkedIn listing with no matching role on the employer's own board — the same shape as Case-009 (Wrench Group), Case-011 (DISCO), and Case-012 (V2Soft).

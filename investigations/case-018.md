# Case-018: I'm green bio-based (brand page, no employer opening)

> **Verdict note:** This is a brand-page case: the advertised "company" is not a company at all but Braskem's "I'm green" bioplastic brand page, the real company's own boards list no security role, and the poster has no tie to Braskem, recruiting, or cybersecurity. The posting's only funnel is direct-messaging the poster.

## Observation

A LinkedIn card under the name "I'm green bio-based" showed a Cybersecurity Analyst role, United States Remote, Full-time, no pay stated. On its face it appeared to qualify: entry-level framing, no required years of professional experience, no required certifications. It was flagged UNVERIFIED by the job watch because the role did not appear on the employer's own LinkedIn jobs tab, there was no apply path, and the poster was not affiliated with the named page. The investigator directed a dig on 2026-11-05.

## Hypothesis

Either a real employer was hiring for a security role through the "I'm green bio-based" page, or the card was an unverifiable posting borrowing a real brand's page, with no opening behind it. Verification checked what the page actually is, the real company's own job board and LinkedIn jobs tab, and the poster's public affiliation.

## Evidence

- **The advertised "company" is a product brand, not an employer.** "I'm green bio-based" is Braskem's brand page for a sugarcane-based bioplastic. It is not a company and has no cybersecurity operation. The page has four attached employees, all in marketing.
- **No security role on the real company's board.** Braskem's real job board lists five openings, all chemistry and sales — nothing in security anywhere. The brand page's own LinkedIn Jobs tab likewise showed no jobs.
- **The posting accepts no applications.** LinkedIn showed "Not currently accepting applications" for the card. There is no Apply or Easy Apply route and no external apply destination.
- **The poster has no tie to the brand, recruiting, or cybersecurity.** The poster is publicly a PhD student in coastal ecosystem economics in Ireland (University of Galway). No affiliation with Braskem, recruiting, or cybersecurity appears anywhere in his public record.
- **The only funnel is a direct message.** The posting exists only on LinkedIn, and its sole response route is direct-messaging the unaffiliated poster — the resume/contact-collection shape seen in earlier cases in this register, with no employer channel behind it.
- **No fee or payment request.** No fee, training, or payment request was found in the posting text.

## Verification

- Posting review: LinkedIn 4485597614 flagged by the job watch's 04:08 EST run on 2026-11-05 — absent from the employer's own LinkedIn Jobs tab, "Not currently accepting applications", poster unaffiliated.
- Page identity check: "I'm green bio-based" resolved to Braskem's bioplastic brand page (four attached employees, all marketing) at dig time, 2026-11-05.
- Employer-board check: Braskem's own job board reviewed at dig time — five openings, all chemistry and sales, no security role.
- Poster check: poster's public profile reviewed — PhD student in coastal ecosystem economics in Ireland, no Braskem, recruiting, or cybersecurity tie.
- No application attempted; no personal information sent.

## Conclusion

**Not verified as a real opening from a real employer — a security posting attached to a bioplastic brand page, with no opening on the real company's board and an unaffiliated poster as its only funnel. Not proven to be a data-harvesting scam.** The evidence establishes a total employer/job mismatch of the same family as Case-010 (Spiralation), where the advertised name was a real program with no security activity and the poster was publicly unaffiliated. As in Case-010, the evidence does not establish who created the posting or why, so the verdict stays at not verified rather than likely fake.

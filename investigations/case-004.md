## Observation

A full-time Easy Apply posting titled "Cyber Security", listed as United States (Remote), appeared under the company name Divine Talent. The posting was written in a staffing style, describing unnamed client environments rather than a role at a named employer.

## Hypothesis

The posting may be a legitimate staffing or coaching business marketing itself, or it may be an unverifiable listing that should not receive personal information. The investigation set out to determine which, without assuming either.

## Evidence

- The posting describes unnamed client environments; no end employer is identified.
- Easy Apply routes only to Divine Talent itself.
- The firm's own website (divinetalent.in, founded 2025) presents a "Career Launchpad" coaching product aimed at international candidates and claims 1,000+ placements.
- The firm's LinkedIn page claims it is a New York staffing partner, while the operation appears to be based in India. This identity mismatch is documented as an inconsistency, not as proof of fraud.

## Verification

- Web searches found no corroboration of the advertised role outside the LinkedIn posting.
- No track record, client list, or independent corroboration of the firm's staffing claims could be found.
- Equally, no evidence was found that the firm had copied another employer's job, impersonated a specific real business, or used a dead domain or an unaffiliated poster, which are the indicators behind the stronger verdicts in Cases 002, 003, 005, and 006.

## Conclusion

**Not verified — skip. Not proven fake.** The firm could not be verified as a legitimate employer for this role, so the posting was not safe to apply to. The evidence does not support calling the firm or the posting fraudulent, and this case is recorded as unverifiable only.

## Action Taken

- Skipped. No application and no personal information submitted.
- Case kept separate from the confirmed impersonation pattern so that future evidence, in either direction, can update the assessment.

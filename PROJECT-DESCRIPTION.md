# Project Description

## Resume Bullets

- Conducted 6 structured OSINT investigations of suspicious cybersecurity job postings (Sept–Oct 2026), producing 1 confirmed scam identification, 4 likely fake / impersonation verdicts, and 1 unverifiable listing, using domain resolution checks, poster affiliation verification, template correlation, and employer-site cross-referencing.
- Correlated indicators across cases to document a repeatable fake-recruitment playbook (impersonated foreign businesses, placeholder page renaming, dead contact domains, rapid posting closure after collecting ~26 applicants per posting) and published findings as a case-file portfolio with an IOC-style indicator table.
- Applied evidence discipline throughout: confirmed facts, indicators, hypotheses, and unverified items recorded separately, graded verdicts with confidence levels, no attribution claimed without evidence, and zero personal information submitted to any suspicious posting.

## LinkedIn Projects Description

Fake Job Recruitment Threat Hunting and OSINT Investigation. During my cybersecurity job search, I investigated six suspicious LinkedIn job postings using the same method I use in SOC work: observe, form a hypothesis, gather evidence, verify it against independent sources, then decide and act. The investigations identified one confirmed scam that copied a real employer's job, four likely impersonation postings built on real foreign businesses with dead contact domains and unaffiliated posters, and one listing that could not be verified and was skipped without being labelled fraudulent. I documented each case as a structured investigation file, correlated the indicators across cases into a shared playbook, and wrote a final report with defensive recommendations for job seekers, platforms, and impersonated businesses. No application was submitted to any suspicious posting, and no personal information was sent.

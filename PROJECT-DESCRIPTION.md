# Project Description

## Resume Bullets

- Conducted 6 structured OSINT investigations of suspicious cybersecurity job postings (Sept–Oct 2026), producing 1 confirmed scam identification supported by a contemporaneous investigation log and employer-site verification, 4 likely fake / impersonation verdicts, and 1 unverifiable listing, using domain resolution checks, poster affiliation verification, template correlation, and employer-site cross-referencing.
- Correlated indicators across cases to document a recurring suspicious-recruitment pattern (real foreign business names or pages, a placeholder company-page display name, non-resolving contact domains in Cases 002 and 005, and documented rapid closure in Cases 002 and 006). Displayed applicant counts were recorded per case rather than generalized: 16 at first sighting in Case-002, 26 in Case-003, and 26 in Case-006; the other case counts are Not Verified.
- Applied evidence discipline throughout: confirmed facts, indicators, hypotheses, and unverified items recorded separately, graded verdicts with confidence levels, no attribution claimed without evidence, and zero personal information submitted to any suspicious posting.

## LinkedIn Projects Description

Fake Job Recruitment Threat Hunting and OSINT Investigation. During my cybersecurity job search, I investigated six suspicious LinkedIn job postings using the same method I use in SOC work: observe, form a hypothesis, gather evidence, verify it against independent sources, then decide and act. The investigations identified one confirmed scam that copied a real employer's job, four likely fake / impersonation postings associated with real foreign businesses, and one listing that could not be verified and was skipped without being labelled fraudulent. The supporting indicators varied by case and are recorded individually; for example, the checked contact domains did not resolve in Cases 002 and 005, while Case-003's contact domain and Case-006's page-control mechanism remain Not Verified. I documented each case as a structured investigation file, correlated the indicators across cases as a shared pattern without claiming single-actor attribution, and wrote a final report with defensive recommendations for job seekers, platforms, and impersonated businesses. No application was submitted to any suspicious posting, and no personal information was sent.

# Evidence and Screenshots

## Status of screenshot evidence

**No screenshots exist as files for these investigations.**

During the investigations (September 30 – October 16, 2026), job postings and company pages were reviewed live, and findings were recorded the same day as dated investigation notes. Screenshots were not retained as image files, and none are fabricated or reconstructed for this repository. Any screenshot file appearing here in the future will be a genuine capture, redacted as described below, with its capture date recorded.

## How the evidence is preserved instead

Each case in `investigations/` preserves:

- The original LinkedIn job URL and job ID (where recorded at investigation time)
- The investigation date
- The advertised company name and the actual business identified
- The publicly listed poster affiliation, where a poster was displayed
- Domain resolution results and website checks, as observed on the investigation date
- Closure status and displayed applicant count, as observed on the investigation date

Where a field was not recorded or could not be confirmed, the case file marks it **"Not Verified"** rather than estimating it.

## Redaction policy (for any future captures)

- Remove or blur all applicant personal information (names of applicants, profile photos, contact details).
- Poster names may remain only where they were publicly displayed on the posting itself and are already part of the documented evidence in the matching case file.
- Do not capture or publish private messages, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- Record the capture date and the source URL alongside every image.

## Source URLs

Source URLs for every case are listed in the metadata table of the corresponding file in `investigations/`, and in `indicators/suspicious-listings.csv`.

# Case-009: Wrench Group

> **Verdict note:** This case differs from every earlier case in the register. The advertised employer is real, the posting ran under the employer's own name, and no impersonation, fabricated identity, or blind staffing indicator was established. What could not be verified is the role itself: at verification time the employer's own applicant tracking system carried no such opening at all. The verdict is "not verified", not "fake" — a stale or recirculating requisition is a documented possibility, and Wrench Group is **not** labelled fraudulent in this investigation.

## Observation

A full-time posting titled "Cybersecurity Analyst", listed in Sarasota, FL with the description headed "Remote | Wrench Group", appeared under the Wrench Group name at $80,000–$90,000/yr plus bonus. The duties were genuine SOC work on the listing's face: Tier 1/2 SOC triage, SIEM / Microsoft Defender / Rapid7 tooling, and incident response, described with "Cyber Fusion Center" language. Experience was worded as "ideally 1–3 years in cybersecurity, SOC, IT support or a related role" — not a required gate — and Security+, Network+, and SC-900 appeared only under an "Even Better If You Have" heading. On the listing alone, the role fit the investigator's search rules, which is why it was sent for employer-site verification before any application, per the standing verify-first rule.

## Hypothesis

The posting may be a genuine Wrench Group opening that had not yet synced to, or had already been removed from, the employer's own applicant tracking system — or it may be a stale requisition recirculating through LinkedIn and aggregators after the real opening closed. The investigation set out to determine whether a live requisition existed on the employer's own system, without assuming either explanation.

## Evidence

- **The employer's own ATS carries no such opening (controlling check).** Wrench Group's official iCIMS board (careers-wrenchgroup.icims.com) showed exactly one live job in an unfiltered search on 2026-10-23: HVAC Maintenance Technician, ID 2026-13740, Collins Comfort Masters, Tempe, AZ, $21–$25/hr.
- **Keyword and category searches on the employer's ATS all came back empty.** Searches for "Cybersecurity Analyst", "Cybersecurity", "Analyst", and "Security", and the Information Technology category filter, each returned "no jobs found".
- **No indexed employer page exists for the role.** A Google site search of the iCIMS domain likewise showed no Cybersecurity Analyst page.
- **A mirror already records the role as gone.** A Learn4Good mirror of the "Remote Cybersecurity Analyst — SOC & Incident Response" listing states the job is no longer listed.
- **The listing text exists only off the employer's system.** Google's indexed snippet and third-party mirrors (ZipRecruiter, JobMesh) corroborate the listing text ("Remote | Wrench Group | $80,000–$90,000 + Bonus", Cyber Fusion Center language), but none of those is the employer's own posting.
- **The text matches an older posting under a different title.** The LinkedIn/aggregator text matches an approximately 8-month-old TealHQ mirror titled "Cyber Fusion Analyst", consistent with a stale or reposted requisition rather than a fresh opening. Whether that older posting was Wrench Group's own genuine role is Not Verified.
- **The employer is real and the posting used its own name.** Unlike Cases 002, 003, 005, and 006, no third-party business was impersonated, no poster affiliation mismatch was documented, and no dead contact domain was involved.

## Verification

- Employer-site check (controlling): careers-wrenchgroup.icims.com unfiltered search, four keyword searches, and the Information Technology category filter — all performed live on 2026-10-23; only the HVAC Maintenance Technician opening (ID 2026-13740) was live.
- Google site index of the iCIMS domain: no Cybersecurity Analyst page found.
- Aggregator mirrors (ZipRecruiter, JobMesh, Learn4Good): listing text corroborated; Learn4Good records the role as no longer listed.
- TealHQ mirror comparison: listing text matches an approximately 8-month-old "Cyber Fusion Analyst" mirror.
- LinkedIn page itself could not be re-read during verification (authwall; session expired; no sign-in attempted) — recorded as a limitation, not worked around.
- The application task stopped at Step 1 verification as instructed: no form was opened, nothing was uploaded, and no personal information was sent.

## Conclusion

**Not verified — skip. Not proven fake.** The advertised role could not be confirmed as a live opening anywhere on the employer's own system, and the strongest available explanation on the documented evidence is a stale or recirculating requisition: the text matches an approximately 8-month-old posting under a different title, and a mirror already records the role as no longer listed. That explanation is recorded as the leading hypothesis, not as an established finding — a genuine opening that was filled and removed before verification, or a sync error between the employer and LinkedIn, cannot be excluded on public evidence. What is established is narrower and sufficient for the decision: with no live requisition on the employer's own ATS, there was nothing verified to apply to.

## Action Taken

- Skipped. No application and no personal information submitted.
- Recorded as a fourth variant in the correlation analysis: a stale / recirculating requisition under a real employer's own name, alongside impersonation of third-party companies (Cases 002, 003, 005, 006, with 001 a copying variant), unverifiable blind staffing posts (Cases 004, 007), and fabricated employer identity (Case-008).

## Observation

A LinkedIn posting titled "Cyber Security Analyst" appeared under the company name Netrolynx AI, advertised as a remote role.

## Hypothesis

The posting may be a copy of a legitimate employer's job, reposted under a different company name to collect applicants.

## Evidence

- The posting text matched a real job at EaglePicher Technologies.
- The genuine EaglePicher role was onsite in Joplin, MO. The Netrolynx AI version advertised the role as remote.
- No application was submitted and no personal information was sent before the mismatch was identified.

## Verification

The role was cross-checked against the real employer's posting. The duties matched EaglePicher Technologies' genuine opening, while the company name and work location in the LinkedIn posting did not.

## Conclusion

**Confirmed scam.** The posting copied a real employer's job and reposted it as a remote role under a different company name.

## Action Taken

- Blocked before any application or information was submitted.
- Netrolynx AI recorded as a known scam source and skipped on sight in later sightings:
  - 2026-10-04: a "Security Engineer" repost (watch #616) that copy-pasted Telos company text.
  - 2026-10-09: a "Cybersecurity Engineer" repost (LinkedIn job 4478061572), skipped on sight.
  - An additional "Cloud Security Engineer" posting under the same name (LinkedIn job 4473936778) appeared in early listings.

## Indicators

- Job text copied from a different, real employer
- Real onsite role relabelled as remote
- Company name does not match the employer that owns the role
- Repeated reposts under the same name after exposure, each copying another company's text

## Observation

A LinkedIn card showed a Network Security Engineer role in the Detroit metro, onsite, at $110,000–$130,000, posted about an hour earlier. The duties read as genuine network security work. Three things blocked a blind application: V2Soft is a staffing firm and no client employer was named; CCNP/CCSP-class certifications were listed with no required/preferred label; and the posting did not state direct hire vs contract.

## Hypothesis

The card may reflect a genuine client opening that closed quickly — or a listing with no live requisition behind it on the poster's own board. Verification set out to find the role on V2Soft's own system and on mirror boards, without assuming either explanation.

## Evidence

- **The LinkedIn posting is gone.** At dig time the LinkedIn page for this posting ID returned invalid or removed.
- **The poster's own board has no such role.** V2Soft's careers board listed 150 jobs; no Network Security Engineer appears anywhere on it. A search of their board for "CCNP" returned zero jobs.
- **No mirror exists.** Indeed, Dice, ZipRecruiter, and CareerBuilder carried no mirror of this role.
- **The one corroborating snippet did not hold up.** A Google snippet mentioned a Dearborn V2Soft role at $53–$58/hr, but the live ZipRecruiter site showed zero V2Soft jobs in Dearborn or Detroit.

## Verification

- LinkedIn posting ID 4482650324: invalid/removed at dig time, 2026-10-25.
- V2Soft careers board: full check, 150 jobs, no matching role; CCNP search = 0.
- Mirror check: Indeed, Dice, ZipRecruiter, CareerBuilder — no mirror.
- No application attempted; no personal information sent; no account created.

## Conclusion

**Not verified — skip. Not proven fake.** With no retrievable posting text anywhere, the client, the contract/direct question, the certification labels, and even the exact city are all unknowable. Same shape as Case-009 (Wrench Group) and Case-011 (DISCO): a card with no presence on the poster's own board.
